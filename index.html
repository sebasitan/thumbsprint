Questions

Before you order.

Can a thumbnail alone get me more views?

A thumbnail raises your click-through rate, so more of the people YouTube shows your video to will click. Views also depend on your content keeping them watching. We promise a better CTR, and your video does the rest.

What do you need from me?

Your video link or topic, the title idea, and any photos or video frames you want used. A clear photo of your face with a strong expression helps a lot.

How does A/B testing work?

YouTube Studio's Test & Compare lets you upload up to three thumbnails for one video. YouTube shows each to different viewers and tells you which one got the most watch time.

Which time zone are you in?

We work from India (IST). If you're in the US or UK, send your video in the evening and the designs are ready when you wake up.

Do you design for Shorts, Reels and TikTok?

Yes. Vertical covers are 1080 × 1920 and we keep faces and text in the area each app shows. YouTube now lets channels upload custom Shorts thumbnails, but A/B testing is only for long-form videos, so the win-or-redo promise covers those.

How do I pay?

PayPal, Wise, or card through Fiverr and Upwork. Monthly clients pay at the start of each month.
